# Use of the PTRN name and marks

The PTRN name, the PTRN logos and icons, and the `.ptrn` file icon (everything in this `brand/` folder except the files in `source/`) identify the PTRN Working Group and the `.ptrn` file format. They are **not** licensed under this repository's license.

A trademark policy is in preparation. Until it is published:

- You may use the files here unmodified to link to or refer to PTRN, the PTRN Working Group or the `.ptrn` format, for example in articles, talks, documentation, or a list of supported file types.
- Please ask first before using the marks on a product, website, app, merchandise or packaging, or to suggest that your software supports or is certified for `.ptrn`.
- Don't modify the marks or combine them with your own logo.

Questions and requests: [hello@ptrnformat.org](mailto:hello@ptrnformat.org)
